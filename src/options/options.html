Volume curve

How the slider position maps to loudness. Human hearing is logarithmic, so a linear slider puts almost all the useful range in the bottom quarter.

Larger range = quieter at low slider positions and finer control near silence. 40 dB is a good default; 50–60 dB suits headphones.

Output = positionn. 2–3 is commonly recommended; 1 is linear.

Output equals slider position — the way YouTube's own slider behaves.
